Tap to play!
Grown-ups: hold the phone how your little one will hold it, then tap. Tilt to pour the sand, tap 🌊 to smooth it and re-level. Press and hold ⚙️ (top corner) for settings.

Grown-up settings

Tilt & shake (motion sensors)
Tilt sensitivity

How far the phone must tip before flat sand slides: about 50° on Low, 42° on Medium, 34° on High (piles and steep walls go a little sooner).

Buried dinosaurs

Hidden buries them deep under flat sand with no lumps to give them away. Changing this washes the sand smooth and buries a new set.

Water in low spots (for moats)

Like digging near the sea: holes and trenches fill with water. Low needs a deep dig; High fills even shallow ones.

Castle keepers

Little builders who live in the big castle and rebuild anything knocked down. They get crosser each time.

Sun

A low sun casts long shadows that make footprints and stamps stand out.
